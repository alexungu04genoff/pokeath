# Pokemon Athletes - smooth board with separate paper artwork

The current physical proof-of-concept is **output/sloped_lap_prototype/**, built by `build_sloped_lap_prototype.py`. Complementary 45-degree lap faces have 0.4 mm vertical / 0.283 mm normal clearance. B's underside rises from a bed-connected foot rather than floating. Canonical sockets, seam and chassis remain unchanged, with the existing 8 mm local tab projection. Eight smooth pin tests and four blank tile tests are included.

For one print session use `output/sloped_lap_prototype/prototype_2x1_ALL_COMPONENTS_A1.stl`, occupying 167.4 x 161 x 12.2 mm. Both bodies print sockets-up; pins print flat-head-down and shaft-up. Tab slopes are predicted near-support-free, with local bore bridges requiring slicer/physical checks. The report, assembly sections and A1 support diagnostics distinguish this prediction from actual slicing. Production quadrants remain unchanged; physical fit and wear must be tested before propagating the lock.

The canonical four-board A1 chassis is **output/canonical_chassis/**, rebuilt by `build_canonical_chassis.py` from `chassis_standard.json`. It uses 38 mm pitch, 36 x 36 mm sockets with 2 mm corner radii and 3 mm depth, 2 mm nominal dividers, a 6 mm exterior track rim and 10 mm thickness. All outside-corner sockets use the same removable tile geometry. Preserved scenery and wide START/finish banners remain flat staging areas. No gameplay tile library is generated.

`output/A1_placement_preview/` contains slicer-style top and angled plate renders plus support diagnostics, generated by `render_a1_placement.py` from unchanged finished STLs. Akala Q4 is the most constrained production section at 229.243 x 229.280 x 10 mm; centering on the 256 mm A1 plate leaves at least 13.36 mm at every edge. Its baked 9.16 degree print rotation is preserved. The complete sloped lap prototype test arrangement is shown in the same style. No brim or generated support footprint is included; see `PLACEMENT_REPORT.md` for limitations.

The consistent fingernail recess at one socket edge is 6 mm wide, extends 1 mm into the divider, and is 1.2 mm deep. This locally leaves 1 mm of divider at the top; the lower divider retains its full width. Tile shape and insertion do not require a particular rotation.

Print `output/canonical_chassis/fit_test/` first on the actual A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle and intended material. The coupon has four identical sockets. Blank 2.8 mm thick test tiles provide 0.15, 0.20, 0.25 and 0.30 mm clearance per side, identified by one to four top dimples. The final tile clearance remains **unselected** in the canonical standard until the physical fit test confirms it.

Every board directory contains four finished print STLs and assembled top/underside previews. `SIZING_REPORT.md` and `finished_stl_bounds.json` report each quadrant's dimensions, socket/rim/seam geometry and lock clearances. Snap keys print separately; use three per board. Mechanical key and cavity dimensions remain absolute millimetres. Internal rail overhangs may need selective supports and cleanup; test the included lock coupons before committing to a full board.

The 40 mm sizing files in **output/final_sizing_40mm/** are superseded and should not be mixed with the canonical chassis.

The earlier **output/paper_art/** kit described below uses a different scale. Its paper PDFs must be regenerated before using them with the new four-board STLs.

## Current design

- Four outline-shaped sections assemble to approximately **320 x 290 mm**, with 0.25 mm seam gaps. All fit the A1 mini bed.
- The base is 8 mm thick. **All raised artwork is removed.** Non-playing scenery areas and the START banner are flat at Z=8 mm.
- All 33 track spaces remain **1.4 mm deep**, with continuous flat floors at Z=6.6 mm. The S09 starting square has no text, arrow grooves, raised lettering or other obstacles in its floor.
- Track corners now have true circular arcs, approximately 10 mm radius, matching the rounded turns. Remaining well corners have 0.8 mm fillets. The finish well has a rounded left end.
- The outline itself is smoothed with a 2 mm physical smoothing scale and sampled every 0.25 mm. It does not merely add polygons to the earlier jagged raster trace. Each well-corner arc uses 48 segments.
- Seams still run between spaces and detour around START. No playing well is split.
- The previous printed snap-lock rails and optional non-slip pad seats remain.

## Snap-lock test and assembly

The outer dovetail rails capture adjacent sections across the seam and vertically. A central 1 mm spring tongue has a ramp and shoulder. It is intended to rise over a 1.2 mm-high crossbar, then drop into 0.8 mm nominal engagement. Press the tongue **upward from underneath** and pull the rail outward to release.

1. Align coupon halves with a 0.25 mm seam and matching entrances.
2. Put the key's wide flange upward in the channel. Insert its solid end first, leaving the forked end outside.
3. Check free movement before the ramp reaches the catch. Do not force it.
4. Verify that the tongue returns, resists withdrawal, and releases when pressed upward.
5. Repeat several times, checking for cracks, whitening, permanent bending and debris before printing the board.

The geometric check verifies seated clearance and a positive stop during a 0.9 mm attempted withdrawal. It does not simulate elasticity, insertion force or fatigue. Keep the board supported during use; do not assume it can be carried by one section.

Assemble sections on a flat surface. K1 enters from the left, K2 from the right and K3 from the bottom. These connect all four sections; the upper vertical seam has no fourth latch. Forked ends extend about 4 mm past the nominal contour. Confirm all catches engage before applying paper artwork.

Optional underside pad seats are 8.2 mm diameter and 0.6 mm deep. Equal-thickness soft adhesive pads up to 8 mm diameter should protrude slightly. Hard PLA disks do not provide equivalent grip. Pads are not included. The eight seats lie beneath S01/S06, S10/S16, S17/S26 and S24/S30. Locks hold sections together; pads address the whole board sliding on the table.

Existing 18 x 14 mm racer bases in `output/stl/` remain usable. Several racers sharing one space may still crowd it.

## Source and checks

`build_printable_board.py` is the current editable generator, renamed from the layered-art generator. Install `requirements-cad.txt` in a Python environment and run it. Original PNGs are unchanged. Old output directories are retained as prior prototypes, not current print files.

The builder checks watertight connected solids, consistent winding, positive volume, bed fit, whole curved wells, flat floors across every well, no raised artwork, small-sticker fit inside curved wells, seated connector clearance and positive latch stops. The three paper pages and model previews are visually inspected.

Material strength, spring action, support removal, print time, filament use and warping remain physically untested. The PDF renderer reports system font-configuration warnings; reviewed pages render correctly. Smooth outlines approximate the original hand-drawn shape rather than reproducing every ink bump.
